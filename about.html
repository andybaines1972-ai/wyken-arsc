The Club

About Wyken ARSC

A friendly, competitive artistic roller skating club in Coventry, West Midlands — affiliated to GB Skate Artistic.

The Sport

Figure skating's cooler cousin — on quad skates

Artistic roller skating is everything you love about ice figure skating, on four-wheeled quad skates. We coach three Olympic-style disciplines recognised by GB Skate Artistic and World Skate.

○

Figures

Precision tracing of circles and turns on painted lines — the discipline that builds flawless edge control and balance.

✦

Freestyle

The crowd-pleaser — jumps, spins and footwork performed to music. Where artistry meets athleticism.

♪

Dance

Set patterns and free dance, solo or in pairs. Rhythm, timing and elegant edges across the floor.

🌱

Start anywhere

No experience needed. Beginners' groups for children and adults run every week.

🏆

Compete & grade

Progress through GBSA grades and into club, regional, national and international competition.

🤝

A real community

A welcoming, family-run club where parents and skaters become friends for life.

🛡️

Safe & insured

DBS-checked coaches, a named safeguarding officer and full club insurance.

Merit & Grading

How skaters progress

Every WARSC skater follows a clear pathway — from their very first roll to national and international competition. Skills are recognised with merit awards and formal grades along the way.

1Learn to RollFirst skates, balance & stopping. Earn beginner merit badges.
2Basic Skills Grades 1–9Progressive skills, badge by badge, building edges, turns & control.
3Grade test & Grade BookTested by a judge — awarded your Grade Book & certificate.
4Discipline grades & competitionFigures, Free & Dance grades, then club → national → international.
🥉

Bronze merit

Early skills mastered — forward skating, gliding and a controlled stop. The first badge for the kit bag.

🥈

Silver merit

Edges, simple turns and backward skating coming together with growing confidence and flow.

🥇

Gold merit

Polished control across the floor — ready to move into formal grade tests and competition skating.
