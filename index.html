The Sport

Figure skating's cooler cousin — on quad skates

Three Olympic-style disciplines, recognised by GB Skate Artistic and World Skate.

○

Figures

Precision edges and turns traced on lines — the foundation of balance and control.

✦

Freestyle

Jumps, spins and footwork to music — where artistry meets athleticism.

♪

Dance

Set patterns and free dance, solo or in pairs — rhythm, timing and elegance.
