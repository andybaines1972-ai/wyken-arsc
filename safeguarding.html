Governance & Safeguarding

Backed by GB Skate Artistic

WARSC is affiliated to GB Skate Artistic (GBSA), the national governing body for artistic roller skating. Official policies, the competition diary, regulations and results all live with GBSA — linked below.

🛡️

Worried about a child or want to raise a concern?

Your safety is our priority. Concerns can be raised in confidence with a club welfare officer, or directly with the GBSA Safeguarding Team. Serious or urgent concerns about a child's safety should always go to the Police (999) or the NSPCC (0808 800 5000).
